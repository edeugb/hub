11 · Growth models — PLG, SLG, and hybrids

11.1 The three motions

SLG (Sales-Led)PLG (Product-Led)Hybrid (Product-Led Sales)
EngineSales team; «buy before you try»; top-down adoptionThe product acquires, activates, and retains; «try before you buy»Self-serve to land, sales to expand into enterprise
ACVHigh; few customers needed for high ARRLow; you need ~10× more customers for the same ARR (at €1,000 ACV → 1,000 customers for €1M ARR)Mid; today's norm in B2B
PM's roleOptimize demo-ability, post-sale time-to-value, expansionPQLs, activation rate, time to «aha moment», free→paid conversionDesign the hand-raisers: usage signals that trigger commercial contact
WhenHigh ACV + buyer far from user + compliance/security in the decisionLow ACV + user = buyerMost WFM/HR tech is SLG with hybrid ambitions

Wes Bush (Product-Led Growth, 2019): uses Van Westendorp for pricing and the «Triple A sprint» for monthly optimization; notes that «over 30% of typical onboarding steps are unnecessary». Bush now describes three eras: PLG 1.0 (user-led), 2.0 (agentic), 3.0 (headless). And a structural observation: it's rare for PLG companies to stay PLG — VC/Wall Street expectations almost always force an eventual move toward Enterprise.

11.2 Growth loops vs funnels

(Balfour/Winters/Kwok/Chen — Reforge, «Growth Loops are the New Funnels».)

  • A funnel empties; a loop feeds itself: closed systems where the output is reinvested into the input, generating compounding effects.
  • Examples: content→SEO→signup→content; user invites user; customer data improves the product for the next customer.
  • Types: acquisition loops (viral, UGC/content, paid with positive LTV/CAC reinvested), engagement loops, etc.
  • The central question: «How does your product grow?» — if five people at your company give different answers, you have a big problem.

11.3 Expansion revenue as the engine

In mature B2B, land & expand > acquisition. Product levers:

  • Seats — growth within the account.
  • Additional modules — cross-sell.
  • Usage tiers — the expansion PM designs the moments where the customer naturally hits their plan's limit.

Practice

Analyze my current company's growth model: what is it today (pure SLG, hybrid)? What loops exist or could exist? Design ONE realistic PLG intervention in a sales-led context (e.g., self-serve onboarding for a module, a premium feature trial, a public calculator that generates leads).

Artifact

  • A written analysis: «The growth model of [company/vertical] and where the levers are».
  • A mini-pitch for a product-led intervention in an SLG context.
