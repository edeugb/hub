## My 5 operating principles

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="01 · Understand before proposing" icon="magnifier">
		Changing systems before knowing why they exist destroys knowledge and creates enemies.
	</Card>
	<Card title="02 · Translate everything into your MRR lever" icon="seti:graphql">
		A feature that doesn't move acquisition, expansion, or retention is a hobby.
	</Card>
	<Card title="03 · With a launched product, read the footprints" icon="document">
		Tickets, usage, sales, churn — the evidence is already there; it just needs clustering.
	</Card>
	<Card title="04 · Without a product, invert the logic" icon="rocket">
		Make your assumptions explicit, rank them by risk, and test the most dangerous one with a RAT, not an MVP.
	</Card>
	<Card title="05 · Never jump to the solution" icon="warning">
		With or without a product. The customer is the expert in their pain; designing the answer is your job.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>

## How I use this hub

- **Consulting / new job** → Modules 2 and 3 are the landing checklist (day 1 to 90).
- **Refreshing knowledge** → every module ends with "Practice" and "Artifact": theory is worthless without application.
- **Interview prep** → Interview bank + the cases as STAR material.
